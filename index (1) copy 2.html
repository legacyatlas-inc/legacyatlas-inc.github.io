The mission

What is LegacyAtlas?

LegacyAtlas helps families around the world hold on to what matters most, and hand it down.

We keep the things that usually slip away: a grandparent's laugh on a voicemail, the advice you only got once, the business idea scribbled on a napkin, the late-night heart-to-heart. Gathered in one place, they become something your family can return to for generations.

  • Memories, ideas and advice
  • Projects, plans and life lessons
  • Heart-to-hearts and everyday guidance
The story

Why I built LegacyAtlas

I lost my uncle unexpectedly, and with him went the glue that held my family together.

Afterward, I realized how few of his stories, sayings and lessons we had actually kept. They lived in people's heads, and people's heads forget.

LegacyAtlas is my answer. It treats core memories with the seriousness they deserve, the way a family protects an heirloom, so no one has to lose the glue again.

How it works

Meet the Historian

The Historian, or LAH, is a 3D-printed device designed to live in your home and hold your family's legacy.

Inside it runs an AI model powered by Atlas, a team of AI helpers working together to turn the stories you share into a legacy that's easy to explore and pass on.

Inside the Historian

Three ways to keep your legacy

1The VaultYour family's virtual scrapbook+

Save voice messages, handwritten notes and photos in one safe place. Hear a loved one's voice again, years from now, exactly as it was.

2The LegacyWisdom worth passing on+

A dedicated space for business plans, life advice and hard-won lessons, written for the people who come after you.

3The HistoryA timelapse of every generation+

Grandparents grow older, friends move on, and kids forget the aunt or uncle who once changed their diapers. The History keeps a living record of each generation growing up, so those connections are never lost.
